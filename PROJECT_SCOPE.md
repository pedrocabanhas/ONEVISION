# Project scope

## Problem

Technology education and open-source participation can feel fragmented or intimidating, especially for people who are just starting. Useful resources may be scattered, overly technical, or difficult to apply in practice.

## Proposed approach

OpenTechLab will organize accessible learning materials alongside small practical examples. The project will prioritize clarity, reproducibility, responsible technology use, and contributions that can be reviewed by the community.

## Initial audience

- People beginning to learn technology and programming
- Students and self-taught learners
- Educators and mentors
- Developers who want to contribute beginner-friendly resources

## Measuring impact

The project will report measurable results only when data is available. Potential indicators include:
- Number of published guides and examples
- GitHub stars, forks, issues, and merged contributions
- Unique visitors or downloads, where reliable analytics are enabled
- Contributors and translations
- Feedback from learners and educators

No impact figures should be claimed until they have been measured and verified.
